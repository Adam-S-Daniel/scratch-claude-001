## 8. Safety & Security

*(P1, 26:47–31:12 — Marnie Heller)*
- Every trip has **at least three staff**; **a security guard on every bus**, plus a guide; staff spread out among the group while walking in cities.
- During free time ("Pizor"), LITs must move in **groups of three (four preferred)** so no one is ever alone.
- **Itinerary/hotel confidentiality:** hotel info is **never shared by email**; teens must **not share hotel info or itinerary with anyone** — even a friend they run into in Europe.
- **Everyone must have an international cell-phone plan**; staying reachable on **WhatsApp** at all times (not dependent on Wi-Fi) is a key safety measure.
- Pickpocketing awareness: keep bags in front of you, don't keep your phone in a back pocket, stay present and aware. *(P1, 29:54)*
- **Synagogue/venue security:** names are submitted and approved in advance; synagogues visited have security. The itinerary was chosen intentionally for cities with strong, active Jewish communities. *(P2, 30:16 — Avi Green)*

## 9. Health & Wellness

*(P1, 31:38–35:48 — Marnie Heller / BBYO)*
- **Record/extreme heat expected in Europe** — wear a hat, **constantly drink water**, apply sunscreen, take breaks, get enough sleep (nap on long bus rides).
- **Unlike camp, LITs self-administer their own medication** — practice now if you're not used to it; set phone alarms. Staff check in morning and evening.
- Bring the **OTC meds you normally use** (e.g., Tums, Advil) — exact equivalents are hard to find overseas. Staff keep a small emergency supply.
- **Culture shock is common** (changing countries/locations every few days). Use your resources: friends, staff, and the **Capital Camps Community Care Team** back home. *(P1, 34:54)*
- **Dietary needs / allergies:** share all needs **ahead of time**; communicate with LIT staff on the ground. Recommended tool: **Equal Eats** allergy cards (printable, translate allergies into local languages). *(P1, 24:53 — BBYO)*

## 14. Q&A Highlights

*(Q&A facilitated by Javi Goldsher, P2, 14:15–37:12)*

1. **Can family visit us in Europe?** Yes, "with an asterisk" — once the itinerary is out you'll know when you're in each country; visits can happen during a **Pizor / free-time / Shabbat** window. **Coordinate directly with LIT staff.** *(P2, 14:48 — Arielle Rosen)*
2. **Will security be armed?** **No — unarmed**, because one guard travels across borders with the group (guns can't cross borders easily). They **will add a local armed guard** if a situation warrants (has been done before). *(P2, 15:44 — Marnie Heller)*
3. **Why only one female counselor when most of the group is female?** Staff team is balanced deliberately; counselors **don't stay in hotel rooms** with campers (as in Max Village / "Carha Teed" [sp?]), so gender breakdown matters less as campers get older. *(P2, 16:28 — Alana; 17:17 — Arielle Rosen)*
4. **Free exploration time in Europe (like "Hofesh")?** Yes — that's **Pizor**: explore city markets/centers in groups of ≥3 with phones, staff, guide, and security nearby; **usually at least once a day.** *(P2, 17:55 — Arielle Rosen)*
5. **Will country guides speak the local language?** Yes — one main guide travels throughout; each country adds a **licensed local guide** (a resident, not 24/7) who speaks the local language. *(P2, 19:05 — Avi Green)*
6. **Are staff on duty 24/7 / do they get time off?** Camper safety is the top priority — campers can reach staff **any time** via phone; staff stay in frequent communication. LITs are asked to **follow curfew/sleep rules** partly so staff also get the rest they need to give good care. *(P2, 19:53 — Ari Geller; 20:39 — Alana)*
7. **What is Shabbat like in Europe?** Time with **local Jewish communities** (a JCC or synagogue) plus the "Capital Camps flair"; **whites** are on the packing list. **Shabbat in Krakow & Prague**; typically a **Kabbalat Shabbat at a local (often Orthodox) synagogue**; **all Shabbat meals are kosher** (Fri dinner, Sat lunch); **no bus on Shabbat** (walking tours / parks). *(P2, 21:51 — Arielle Rosen; 22:28 — Avi Green)*
8. **Can a family friend drop off on July 5 instead of a parent?** Yes — just **notify staff in advance**; the LIT must have all the right documents. *(P2, 24:04 — Alana)*
9. **Can I request NOT to room with someone?** Staff want to know what's on your mind, but **flexibility/openness** is part of the experience; roommates change every 1–3 nights ("a big puzzle"). A **true personal need** can be discussed, but **specific avoid-this-person requests are not guaranteed.** *(P2, 24:56 — Ari Geller / Alana)*
10. **Is there adjustment time when LITs return to camp?** Yes, **100%** — the **two days after the July 20 return are planned as chill/relaxation days**; campers who want to jump into programming can, others can rest. *(P2, 27:51 — Ari Geller / Alana)*
11. **Modesty/attire (given Orthodox synagogues)?** Dress **more modestly than at camp** when in places like Old Town Prague or **Kazimierz** in Krakow: men in pants + polo/button-down; girls with shoulders covered, no bare midriff, ~knee-length skirts. Otherwise no strict dress code — just don't draw attention and dress smart for the heat. *(P2, 28:42 — Avi Green)*
12. **Do the European congregations have extra security?** Yes — names submitted/approved in advance; synagogues have security. The chosen cities have strong, active Jewish communities ("more Hebrew than English in the streets of Budapest"). *(P2, 30:16 — Avi Green)*
13. **When will the itinerary be shared?** A **few weeks before the trip** (still being finalized; subject to change). Treat it as a **guide, not a checklist** — you'll be notified of any major change (city or hotel). *(P2, 31:37 — Alana; 32:41 — Marnie Heller)*
14. **When will LITs know the full roster?** Via the pre-trip **group chat (likely WhatsApp)** that starts before the trip; you can also see names on the Zoom call. *(P2, 34:15 — Alana)*
15. **Passport validity rule?** **Must be valid for at least 3 months** after the return (Avi first said 6 months, then corrected to 3; 6 months is the *Israel* rule). **Double-check** you have the right, unexpired passport. *(P2, 35:32 — Avi Green; 36:03 — Marnie Heller)*
